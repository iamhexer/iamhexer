## Hexer

Co-founder and CEO of [Hexerize](https://hexerize.com), a technology company from Ghana building useful apps for everyone. I lead product and engineering across our apps.

### What we're building

- **[Piazam](https://piazam.com)**: Ghana's pre-order store for fashion, electronics and home goods, ordered factory-direct from China.
- **[DropAsecret](https://dropasecret.com)**: anonymous messages through your own link. Reply back and forth, and reveal names only when both people agree.
- **[HexerSMS](https://hexerize.com/apps/hexersms/)**: school management for nursery, primary and JHS schools in Ghana. In development.

I also write on the [Hexerize blog](https://hexerize.com/blog/) about the apps and how they work.
